# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- The "Uninstall" item is now plain text in red (no red background).
- Apps that cannot be removed show a grey "Uninstall (unavailable)" item. Clicking it explains why (desktop file, Exec line, detection notes).

## 0.2.0
- Multi-distro support: dpkg (Debian, Ubuntu, Mint), rpm (Fedora, openSUSE), Snap.
- Steam games (uses Steam's own uninstall dialog).
- AppImage support (files are moved to the Trash).

## 0.1.0
- First version: pacman/AUR and Flatpak.
